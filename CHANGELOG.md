### Added

- Booking, look-up, cancel and reschedule by reference; branch and appointment-type catalogue; availability by branch-local date.
- Notifications through a transactional outbox, a simulated channel, an optional SMTP channel (Mailpit for local use) and reminders.
- Angular web app: booking wizard, confirmation with calendar file, manage page with messages.
- CI pipeline, image checks, release pipeline, end-to-end browser tests.
